About ANAS CITY 3D

ANAS CITY 3D is an original browser game project created by Anas Ali. It is built as a lightweight 3D game that can be played on a computer or phone through a modern web browser.

What you can do

Explore the city, move around the streets, jump and collect coins. The game is being developed further with new gameplay features planned for future versions.
