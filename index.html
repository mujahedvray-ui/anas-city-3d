How to play

PC: WASD or arrow keys to move. Press Space to jump.

Collect the coins and try to get a perfect score.

About the game

ANAS CITY 3D is an original browser game project created by Anas Ali. The game is designed for quick play on computers and mobile devices, with a 3D city, collectible coins, movement controls and jumping.

Play on your browser

No installation is required. Open the game page, keep your internet connection on so the 3D engine can load, and start playing.
